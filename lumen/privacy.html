LUMEN privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Google Drive

If you choose to copy recordings to Google Drive, LUMEN asks Google for the drive.file permission only. With it, LUMEN can create a "LUMEN Recordings" folder in your Drive and upload your recordings into it. It cannot see, read or change any other file in your Drive.

Your Google sign-in is stored encrypted on your TV only. It is never sent to the app's developer or to anyone else. Your recordings go straight from your TV to your own Drive.

You can sign out in LUMEN (Settings, Recordings, Copy to Google Drive, Sign out), which also cancels LUMEN's access with Google. You can also remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Other data

LUMEN keeps your settings, provider logins, favourites and recordings on your TV. It has no servers of its own and does not collect analytics or share data with third parties.
